@descriptions
desc.dashboard = Create and select missions and see the computed status of the active plan.
desc.map = Rotatable 3D Mars globe with real NASA imagery and landing sites (click Jezero → local 3D terrain), interactive 2D map with NASA Mars Trek base maps and scientific layers, and 3D terrain from real elevations.
desc.terrain = DEM derivatives: slope, aspect, curvature, roughness, relief, variability and complexity, with profiles and quality checks.
desc.dem = Load, inspect and validate the Jezero DEM (USGS CTX 20 m) or your own GeoTIFF.
desc.route = Four route strategies computed with A*, Weighted A* or Dijkstra on a 16-neighbour terrain graph.
desc.hazards = Rule-based detection of steep, rough, rocky, crater-edge, discontinuous and unknown terrain.
desc.ai = Supervised terrain-traversability model (trained and tested here), computer vision on surface images, unsupervised terrain units and an explanation assistant.
desc.autonav = GPS-free localisation: wheel odometry, gyroscope, visual odometry and landmarks fused by an EKF.
desc.navintel = Accuracy and uncertainty of navigation configurations compared by Monte Carlo.
desc.comm = Radio link budget and terrain line-of-sight between the crew and the base.
desc.relay = Orbiter contact windows and store-and-forward data flow.
desc.spice = Mars time, season and Sun position (Mars24), with NASA SPICE when kernels are present.
desc.meda = Import and analyse MEDA-style weather data: statistics, anomalies and EVA environment level.
desc.suit = Physics-based simulation of suit consumables with alerts and anomaly detection.
desc.oxygen = Oxygen budget of the planned EVA, depletion forecast and minimum return reserve.
desc.energy = Rover traction energy, power budget and battery state along each route.
desc.timeline = Mission phases, Gantt chart and resources over time.
desc.science = Catalog of science targets, linked to the route.
desc.simulation = Step-by-step replay of the plan under normal and emergency scenarios.
desc.risk = Likelihood × impact matrix computed from the platform's results.
desc.ingest = Safe upload and validation of GeoTIFF, XML/PDS4, CSV, JSON/GeoJSON and images.
desc.provenance = Origin, class and quality of every dataset in use.
desc.reports = Mission report in PDF, HTML, CSV, JSON and Excel.
desc.i18n = Central translation service with 18 languages, fallback and right-to-left support.
desc.settings = Language, theme, units, map and paths.
desc.diagnostics = Libraries, modules, data, resources, tests and logs.
desc.integration = Runs all modules for one start/goal pair and explains the recommended route.
@limitations
lim.dashboard = KPIs are only as good as the models behind them; synthetic or simulated inputs are labelled.
lim.map = Base maps are low-resolution global mosaics; the browser's scale tools assume Earth and are not used.
lim.terrain = A 50 m grid cannot show boulders or small scarps; real local slopes are steeper.
lim.dem = Only the analysis window is resampled; vertical accuracy depends on the source DEM.
lim.route = Routes are optimal only for the chosen cost model and grid; they are not certified traverse plans.
lim.hazards = Thresholds are planning assumptions; rocks smaller than a cell are invisible to this method.
lim.ai = The heuristic detector is not a trained model; YOLO results exist only with real trained weights.
lim.autonav = Sensor data are simulated; real wheel slip and terrain effects can be larger.
lim.navintel = Monte Carlo statistics describe the simulation model, not a real rover.
lim.comm = Preset link values are illustrative; multipath, antenna patterns and dust storms are simplified.
lim.relay = Orbits are synthetic two-body circles, not NASA ephemerides.
lim.spice = Without kernels the analytic Mars24 algorithm is used (accuracy ~0.01° in Ls).
lim.meda = The sample dataset is synthetic; EVA bands are planning assumptions.
lim.suit = Generic suit model; not connected to any real NASA suit.
lim.oxygen = Earth physiology scaled to Mars; real suited metabolic costs vary widely.
lim.energy = Rover parameters are illustrative; thermal and night-time loads are simplified.
lim.timeline = Timing follows the model; operational constraints are not included.
lim.science = Approximate coordinates must be verified in NASA Mars Trek.
lim.simulation = Events and decision rules are simplified flight-rule examples.
lim.risk = Scores depend on documented thresholds and assumptions; they are not safety certification.
lim.ingest = Validation checks format and structure, not scientific correctness.
lim.provenance = Class labels are only as accurate as the information given at import.
lim.reports = Reports repeat model results and their limitations; they are not mission approval documents.
lim.i18n = Translations were prepared with AI assistance and should be reviewed by native speakers.
lim.settings = Some changes take effect after the next calculation.
lim.diagnostics = Checks cover this computer only.
lim.integration = The recommendation is decision support for a prototype, not real astronaut safety guidance.
